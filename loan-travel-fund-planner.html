    Import a file

    Download transactions from your bank or card site and drop the file here. Works with CSV, TSV or TXT, Excel (.xlsx), OFX, QFX, QBO and QIF. Nothing leaves this device.

    Customs

    Declare each transaction. Best guesses are filled in for you; anything you change is marked as yours. Use "Always" to make a rule for similar transactions.
